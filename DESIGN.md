# Sienna theme design notes

The Sienna theme is a warm, dusk-like alternative to RAVEN’s original neon glitch identity. Burnt sienna fills the whole atmosphere rather than appearing as a timid accent. Cream typography, translucent dark-terra panels and thin white outlines keep long personal copy readable.

The hero uses large elliptical “string” lines and generous space to evoke an acoustic instrument without turning the page into artist fandom. Its scroll cue is a fixed fine thread with a slow warm shimmer rather than a bouncing marker. Controls become compact matte pills, while the music visualizer uses two filled, tapered ribbons driven by broad, temporally smoothed audio envelopes. Sienna and Glitch retain their own palettes without returning to frequency bars or frame-by-frame brightness flashes. In Sienna, random jolts, scanlines and glitch bursts are suppressed; switching back restores their original intent elsewhere on the page.

The switch is persistent, keyboard-focusable and updates its accessible label/state. Mobile tab rows scroll horizontally instead of crushing labels, and main surfaces tighten to the viewport at 360–390px widths.
