# RAVEN BIO — product context

## Purpose
Personal bio, links and listening experience for RAVEN. This is not an Ed Sheeran fan site and must not add invented artist content.

## Confirmed direction
- Keep every piece of existing identity and content: profile copy, links, two photos, ten tracks, lyrics, tabs, subtabs, player, collapsible detail and easter egg.
- Add a second runtime-selectable theme, with **Sienna as the default** and the original black/pink glitch theme preserved.
- Remember the visitor’s selection when storage is available; storage failure must not block the page.
- Sienna uses burnt sienna, an intimate ginger/acoustic mood, matte rounded controls with white outlines, newer-Telegram-like clarity, readable warm off-white text and gentle musical waves.
- Switching theme must not reload the page or restart audio.
- Motion must respect reduced-motion preferences and page visibility.

## Delivery
A self-contained replacement `index.html`. Existing relative paths `photo1.jpg`, `photo2.jpg`, and `music/*` remain unchanged.
